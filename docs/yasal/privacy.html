HOMEZY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31.10.2026

In short: Your home data lives on your phone first. If you create an account it is backed up. When you scan a photo or chat with Moka, what you send is passed to an AI provider (Anthropic) to produce a reply; we do not store it. Your card details never reach us. We do not sell your data. You can delete your account and export your data inside the app.

The controller of your data (KVKK / GDPR / LGPD) is Melih Tuğyan (individual, Turkey). Contact: info@themeikai.com. This policy covers the Homezy mobile app (iOS and Android).

3. AI features

  • Photo scan and Moka chat are powered by Anthropic's Claude models. Your content is passed to Anthropic only while your request is processed. We do not store photos or chat text on our servers; we only count how many scans and messages you made.
  • For outfit photos, no face or body information is extracted or stored; the photo is used only to recognise clothing. Try to keep faces out of the frame.
  • Under Anthropic's commercial API terms, data is processed to provide the service and prevent abuse and is not used to train its models by default. See anthropic.com/legal/privacy.
  • Moka is an AI, not a person. Its answers can be wrong and are not medical, psychological, legal or financial advice. If you are thinking of harming yourself or are in an emergency, do not rely on Moka: call your local emergency number right away (112 in Turkey and the EU, 988 in the US, 188 in Brazil) or contact a professional.
  • Outfit suggestions do not use AI; they are computed on your phone.
  • You never have to use these features, and you can withdraw consent later (Profile › Privacy and legal).

4. Who we share data with

Only processors needed to run the service. We do not sell your data or use it for our own advertising.

  • Supabase Inc.: account, backup, points, photo backup, counters (EU region).
  • Anthropic, PBC (US): photo scans and Moka chat.
  • RevenueCat Inc. (US), Apple and Google: subscriptions and payments. Card details go only to Apple / Google.
  • Google AdMob (US / Ireland): the banner ad on the free plan.
  • Open-Meteo (Europe): weather lookup.
  • Authorities, if legally required.

5. International transfers

Anthropic, RevenueCat and Google are outside Turkey and the EU. Transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses (GDPR Art. 46, KVKK Art. 9) and on your explicit consent. Starting a chat or a scan is your explicit consent to that transfer.

6. Retention

  • Data on your phone: until you uninstall the app.
  • Account and backup: while your account exists. When you delete your account, your account, backup, points and counter records, shared-goal contributions and backed-up photos are permanently deleted right away.
  • RevenueCat and store records: as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Photo and chat content: not stored by us; stored by Anthropic only as long as its own policy says.

7. Your rights

Under GDPR (Art. 15-22), KVKK (Art. 11), LGPD (Art. 18) and CCPA/CPRA you may access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, port your data and withdraw consent. You may complain to your data protection authority (Turkey: KVKK Board; EU: your local authority; Brazil: ANPD; California: Attorney General).

  • Delete: Profile › Delete my account in the app. If you cannot open the app, see the account deletion page.
  • Export: Profile › Privacy and legal › Export my data (a JSON file of your home data).
  • Other requests: email info@themeikai.com; we reply within 30 days.
  • Ads: ads are not personalised. You can reset your advertising ID in your phone settings. There are no ads in Premium.
  • California and other US states: we do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, so no "Do Not Sell or Share" link is needed. We do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.
  • Brazil (LGPD): the controller and contact details are above; use the same address to exercise your rights.

8. Security

Connections use HTTPS. Server tables are protected with row-level access rules so each user reaches only their own data. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach occurs we will notify you and the authority within the legal deadlines.

9. Children

Homezy is not designed for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Where the age of digital consent in your country is higher than 13 (for example 16 in some EU countries), you need a parent's permission. If we learn a child under 13 has an account we will delete it; please tell us if you notice one.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we update the date above and announce important changes in the app.
